The Problem We Solve

Managing shortlets is hard.
Staynexa makes it simple.

Most shortlet operators juggle spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper records. Staynexa brings everything into one intelligent platform.

Without Staynexa
  • Bookings tracked in WhatsApp & spreadsheets — double-booking risk
  • Guest payment records scattered across phone calls and receipts
  • Housekeeping teams notified manually — rooms not ready on time
  • No visibility into which properties are actually profitable
  • Maintenance issues reported verbally — forgotten or delayed
  • End-of-month reporting takes days to compile manually
  • Staff accountability is hard to track without a proper system
With Staynexa
  • Centralized booking system with real-time availability & conflict detection
  • All payments logged, tracked, and balanced automatically per booking
  • Auto-tasks created for housekeeping on every checkout — instant alerts
  • Live revenue, occupancy, and profit dashboards per property and outlet
  • Maintenance logged with photos, priority, and technician assignment
  • One-click financial reports — daily, monthly, or any custom period
  • Role-based access — every staff member sees exactly what they need
Platform Features

Everything you need to run any shortlet operation

Nine core modules — fully integrated and built for the realities of hospitality management.

Booking Management
Track reservations, check-ins, check-outs, cancellations, and booking history. Handle discounts, caution fees, and multi-channel bookings.
Property Management
Manage multiple properties, apartments, rooms, and units from one place. Assign branches, set rates, and monitor availability in real time.
Guest Management & CRM
Maintain detailed guest profiles, booking history, communication records, and preferences to build lasting relationships.
Payment Tracking
Monitor payments, outstanding balances, caution fee deposits, and full transaction history with automatic receipt generation.
Housekeeping Management
Schedule cleaning tasks, assign staff, track room readiness, and manage laundry — automatically triggered on every checkout.
Maintenance Management
Log issues with photos and priority levels, assign technicians, track repairs, and auto-link fix costs to the expense ledger.
Staff Management
Manage staff profiles, attendance, role-based access, salary records, loans, and departmental performance.
Reports & Analytics
Access powerful dashboards, financial summaries, occupancy rates, and exportable reports — daily, monthly, or custom range.
Automated Notifications
Send booking confirmations, payment receipts, check-in guides, checkout reminders, and operational alerts automatically.
